A short stick-figure fight scene from the early 2000s. The whole thing is hand-drawn frame-by-frame in Flash MX, no tweens.

## Notes in retrospect

The pacing is too slow in the first half. The original timeline had 24fps but a lot of frames were duplicated — should have been 12fps holds.

The kick at the end is what made me want to keep doing this.

# Design inspiration — experimental / avant-garde web

Reference gallery of experimental, avant-garde, and toy-like websites to study
for chaipalaka.com's design direction. Not prescriptive — a study set.

**Provenance:** assembled 2026-06-14 via two `deep-research` workflow passes
(fan-out web search → fetch → adversarial verification → synthesis), then every
URL reachability-checked by hand (`curl`, HTTP 200 unless noted). Entries that
could not be confirmed reachable were dropped.

**Caveat:** experimental and personal sites are volatile — re-check any URL
before relying on it. Studio sites (Lusion, Active Theory, Immersive Garden)
relaunch often, so the exact visual may drift from the note. Notes are
visual/conceptual only — no implementation detail by design.

---

## 1 · Playful / interactive / toy-like

Closest to the planned physics-card / draggable-tether direction — sites that
behave like a toy.

- **Neal.fun** — https://neal.fun/ — A whole portfolio of tactile single-purpose
  toys ("games and stuff"); each a self-contained interactive idea. See **The
  Password Game** (https://neal.fun/password-game/) — absurd escalating rules
  that fight you — and **Infinite Craft** (https://neal.fun/infinite-craft/),
  drag-to-combine elements. _(Sub-pages bot-challenge scripts but are live in a
  real browser.)_
- **Patatap** — https://patatap.com/ — Press A–Z and each key fires a
  synchronized animated shape + sound; spacebar swaps the whole palette. Pure
  audiovisual instrument. _(Flashing-image warning.)_
- **Pointer Pointer** — https://pointerpointer.com/ — Move your mouse and a
  photo of a person literally pointing at your cursor appears. One joke,
  executed perfectly.
- **tixy.land** — https://tixy.land/ — Write a ≤32-character math function and a
  16×16 grid of dots animates from it. Generative coding as a minimalist toy.
- **This Is Sand** — https://thisissand.com/ — Pour streams of colored sand that
  pile and slide into layered sandscapes. Meditative, tactile, shareable.
- **Paper Toilet** — https://papertoilet.com/ — Rafaël Rozendaal's endless
  toilet-paper roll that unspools as you scroll. A scroll-toy _and_ canonical
  net.art (also §4).
- **Koalas to the Max** — https://www.koalastothemax.com/ — Cursor-driven
  quadtree subdivision: colored dots keep splitting as you sweep, slowly
  revealing a hidden image. Calming generative reveal.
- **Drawing Garden** — https://drawing.garden/ — Click-drag and a meandering
  trail of fruit, flowers and foliage grows under your cursor, over ambient
  music.
- **Pixels Fighting** — https://pixelsfighting.com/ — Two colors endlessly flip
  neighboring pixels, fighting for territory that never resolves. Hypnotic
  cellular loop.
- **Find the Invisible Cow** — https://findtheinvisiblecow.com/ — Empty screen;
  a voice yells louder as your cursor nears the hidden cow. Audio hot-and-cold.
  _(Loud.)_
- **Cursor Effects** — https://cursoreffects.com/ — Tim Holman's menu of
  attachable pointer trails (rainbows, snow, springy text). A catalog of
  cursor-play ideas.
- **Nyan Cat** — https://www.nyan.cat/ — The rainbow pop-tart cat looping
  through space with chiptune + an elapsed-seconds counter. The archetypal
  single-serving meme toy.
- **Constraint Systems** — https://constraint.systems/ — Sam Wray's hub of
  experimental browser tools (glitch/tile/image editors) in a spare monospace
  shell; each a small constrained interactive system.

## 2 · WebGL / 3D immersive

- **Bruno Simon** — https://bruno-simon.com/ — Drive a toy car around a 3D world
  to discover his work. The canonical game-as-portfolio (CSS Design Awards
  _Website of the Year_ 2019).
- **Lusion** — https://lusion.co/ — Shader- and scroll-driven 3D storytelling
  studio; among the most-awarded immersive sites (Awwwards + CSSDA site-of-year
  2023).
- **Igloo Inc** — https://www.igloo.inc/ — Infinite-scroll WebGL world of icy 3D
  scenes, smooth spatial transitions (Awwwards _Developer_ Site of the Year
  2024).
- **Active Theory** — https://www.activetheory.net/ — Long-running creative
  studio; their own site is a rotating showcase of immersive, game-engine-grade
  web experiences.
- **Resn** — https://resn.co.nz/ — Mischievous, character-driven WebGL studio;
  playful and weird at high production polish.
- **Immersive Garden** — https://immersive-g.com/ — Paris studio, cinematic
  3D/WebGL for luxury brands; lush, fluid, motion-forward.
- **Aristide Benoist** — https://aristidebenoist.com/ — Creative-dev portfolio
  with cinematic WebGL image transitions; moody and immersive.
- **Robin Noguier** — https://robin-noguier.com/ — Interaction designer; smooth
  scroll-driven project reveals with tactile hover/transition motion.
- **Yuta Abe** — https://yutaabe.com/ — Tokyo front-end dev; clean 3D portfolio
  surfaced on Awwwards' Three.js gallery.
- **Enzo Casalini** — https://enzo-casalini.dev/ — Creative-dev portfolio built
  as an immersive 3D scene with generative visuals.
- **Ting Yan** — https://tingyan.me/ — "Portfolio as conversation": the site
  unfolds as a chat-style interaction rather than a page.
- **100,000 Stars** — https://stars.chromeexperiments.com/ — Fly through a
  navigable 3D map of our stellar neighborhood, Sun out to the galaxy.
  Data-as-space.

## 3 · Typographic / editorial / brutalist

- **David Rudnick** — https://davidrudnick.org/ — Typeface designer's portfolio:
  monochrome high-contrast grid, vast whitespace, type _is_ the interface.
  Severe and avant-garde.
- **Bureau Borsche** — https://bureauborsche.com/ — Munich studio; systematic
  editorial grid, elegant whitespace, contemporary type-driven minimalism.
- **Studio Feixen** — https://studiofeixen.ch/ — Swiss studio that refuses one
  house style — saturated color, playful type, every project its own visual
  language.
- _(Directory)_ **Brutalist Websites** — https://brutalistwebsites.com/ —
  Curated index of raw, anti-comfortable web design; the canonical feed for this
  whole bucket.

## 4 · Net.art / generative / retro-web revival

- **Cameron's World** — https://www.cameronsworld.net/ — A web collage stitched
  from thousands of archived GeoCities pages — animated GIFs, spinning logos,
  "under construction" chaos. "A love letter to the Internet of old."
- **JODI** — https://wwwwwwwww.jodi.org/ — Canonical 1995 net.art: green-on-black
  dense ASCII/glitch with no nav, no prose — form over readability as a
  statement.
- **Windows93** — https://www.windows93.net/ — A browser parody of a mid-90s
  desktop OS: draggable icons, fake apps, terminal, retro games. Full desktop
  metaphor as art piece.
- **Poolsuite** — https://poolsuite.net/ — Retro-Mac/CRT skeuomorphic internet
  radio with a fake boot-screen; "ultra-summer" vaporwave-adjacent nostalgia
  (formerly Poolside FM).
- **Yehwan Song** — https://yehwansong.com/ — "Anti-friendly web": deliberately
  introduces friction into seamless UI to expose the web's hidden conventions.
  Conceptual critique as interface.
- **Rafaël Rozendaal — Index** — https://www.newrafael.com/websites/ — The
  artist's own catalog of ~93 single-serving net.art sites, each its own domain.
  A deep well to keep mining. Verified live samples:
  - https://www.onlysuddenly.com/
  - https://www.intotime.com/
  - https://www.ifnoyes.com/
  - https://www.fallingfalling.com/
  - https://www.muchbetterthanthis.com/
  - https://www.deepsadness.com/
  - Each one minimal full-screen concept (a single color field, motion, or
    gesture) sold as art yet free to visit.
- **Zombo.com** — https://zombo.com/ — _Caveat:_ the iconic "you can do anything"
  intro is gone — as of Feb 2026 it's a degraded "New Management" placeholder
  stuck at "Loading… 0%." History/cautionary, not the classic experience.

## 5 · Designer / developer personal sites — closest reference class

The reference class most directly analogous to chaipalaka.com.

- **Jack Wild** — https://www.isjackwild.com/ — Creative technologist;
  "functional design with plenty of personality and playful interaction."
- **Jesper Landberg** — https://jesperlandberg.com/ — Independent creative dev;
  scroll-driven, motion-rich, repeatedly Awwwards-recognized.
- **Rauno Freiberg** — https://rauno.me/ — Exemplar of restrained-but-tactile
  craft: micro-interactions, keyboard nav, obsessive detail. Strong model for a
  quiet-yet-alive personal site.
- **Lynn Fisher** — https://lynnandtonic.com/ — Famous for a site that redesigns
  itself at every breakpoint, packed with responsive easter eggs. Resize the
  window — it rewards you.
- **Josh Comeau** — https://www.joshwcomeau.com/ — Playful dev-blog with
  whimsical hover/interaction flourishes on otherwise readable content.
- **Amelia Wattenberger** — https://wattenberger.com/ — Interactive, illustrated,
  scrollytelling essays where the diagrams _are_ the argument.
- **Tim Holman** — https://tholman.com/ — Creator of The Useless Web & Cursor
  Effects; personal site stuffed with interactive experiments and easter eggs.
- **Maggie Appleton** — https://maggieappleton.com/ — A hand-illustrated "digital
  garden" — sketches and diagrams, warm and notebook-like, anti-corporate.
- **Henry Desroches** — https://henry.codes/ — Loud, idiosyncratic, retro-tinged
  personal site that treats the portfolio as personality.
- **Koen Vendrik** — https://kvendrik.com/ — Developer site noted for hidden
  easter-egg interactions that reward poking around.

## How to read this for the physics-card site

literally physics-based:

- **§1 (toy/sandbox feel)** — the tactile, one-idea-executed-fully sensibility.
- **Rozendaal single-serving pieces (§4)** — one idea, full-screen, zero chrome.
- **Rauno Freiberg / Lynn Fisher (§5)** — restrained craft + reward-on-interaction.
